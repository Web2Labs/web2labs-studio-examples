# 04 - Gaming Montage

Edit gameplay recordings with aggressive dynamic zoom, fast pacing, and gaming-style energy. Built for streams, matches, and gameplay highlights.

## When to Use

- You recorded a gaming session or match and want a highlight reel
- You stream on Twitch and want to turn VODs into YouTube content
- You want dynamic zoom punch-ins on key moments

## What You Get

- Edited video with fast-paced cuts and dynamic zoom punch-ins
- Gaming-optimized pacing (keeps action, removes downtime)
- Shorts with the most intense moments
- Subtitles and transcription

## What Makes Gaming Different

The `gaming` preset adjusts AI behavior:
- **Faster cut cadence** — less tolerance for slow moments
- **More zoom punch-ins** — 2-3x more zooms than the default
- **Action priority** — AI favors high-energy moments for shorts
- **Commentary awareness** — keeps narration and callouts, cuts idle gameplay

## Preset Used

`gaming` — Dynamic zoom and gaming-style pacing. Higher energy, faster cuts.
